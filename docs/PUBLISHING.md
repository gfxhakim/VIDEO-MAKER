# Posting your clips

AutoClip Studio does not connect to your social accounts. You post each clip yourself in the official app, which is
exactly what a normal creator does, so nothing about the upload looks automated.

## The flow

1. Open a project (or **Clips** for every project) and click a clip.
2. **Approve** the good ones. Edit the title, hook or hashtags in the **Edit** tab if you want, and re-render if you changed the hook card.
3. In the **Publish** tab, pick the platform. You get the caption, hashtags, and for YouTube the title, each with a **Copy** button.
4. On a computer: **Download**, then upload in TikTok Studio, Instagram, YouTube Studio or Meta Business Suite and paste the caption.
   On a phone: **Share to app** sends the video straight to the TikTok, Instagram or YouTube app and copies the caption at the same time.
5. Press **Mark posted** so the clip shows which platforms it is already on. The **Clips** page has a "Not posted yet" filter.

To grab a whole batch at once, use **Download approved** on the project page. The ZIP holds every MP4 plus a text file of captions per clip.

## Keeping accounts healthy

Reach drops ("shadowbans") usually come from the content and the pattern, not from how the file got there:

- Post the **same clip to many accounts** or re-post near-identical clips: avoid it. Re-style or pick different moments for each account.
- **Bursts**: spread posts out (for example 1 to 3 a day per account) instead of 20 at once.
- **Copyrighted music**: use songs you have rights to; platforms mute or limit clips that match copyrighted audio.
- **Watermarks** from other apps: AutoClip renders clean files, so keep it that way.
- Write a caption that fits the platform: the kit already keeps hashtags to a few per platform and adds `#shorts` for YouTube.
